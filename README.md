## `01` — THE AUTHOR

I'm a developer exploring the ever-expanding world of technology, driven by curiosity about **how things actually work beneath the surface**.

I enjoy taking things apart, understanding their underlying mechanisms, experimenting with different approaches, and adapting to new environments and technologies. I try not to simply use a tool because it works — whenever possible, I want to understand **why it works, where it breaks, and what happens underneath**.

I approach the things I build with a researcher's mindset: **investigate, experiment, test, refine**.

Currently exploring everything from full-stack development and data analysis to machine learning, algorithms, and large language models.

I'm also interested in contributing to **open source**, learning from other developers, and eventually giving back to the projects and communities that have helped me along the way.
